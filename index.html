⚡ Focus 25 Guide

How to Play & Modes

👁️ Eye Scan Mode Visual Speed
  • No touching: Keep hands off and trace numbers 1 to 25 with your eyes only.
  • Timer Countdown: Clock ticks down while you scan as far as possible.
  • Self-Assessment: When time's up, report the highest number reached to get your Focus Rating.
👆 Tap Mode Hand-Eye Speed
  • Physical click: Tap numbers 1 → 2 → 3... in ascending order.
  • Live Feedback: Correct tiles dim with chime tones; wrong tiles shake.
  • Auto Time Tracking: Records your exact speed in seconds when clearing all 25.
💡 Pro Tip: Focus on the center tile to engage your peripheral vision instead of scanning row-by-row!
🐒 Working Memory Guide

How to Play Chimp Test

1
Memorize Numbers:

Numbers 1 to N appear scattered across the 5x5 grid. Take a mental snapshot of where they are.

2
The Mystery Vanish:

When you tap #1, all other numbers instantly turn into blank white squares!

3
Tap from Memory:

Tap the blank squares in ascending numerical order (2 → 3 → 4...) purely from memory.

4
3 Lives & Progression:

You have 3 Lives (❤️ ❤️ ❤️). Each completed stage adds more scattered numbers!

💡 Pro Tip: Before tapping #1, trace the path 1 → 2 → 3 in your mind for 1 second.
🧩 Spatial Memory Guide

How to Play Pattern Memory

1
Watch the Flash:

A random pattern of glowing tiles illuminates for 1.8 seconds, then flips dark.

2
Recreate the Matrix:

Tap the exact squares that were lit. Correct tiles turn green; wrong tiles trigger a strike (❤️).

3
Grid Expansion:

As you beat levels, the grid expands from 3x3 → 4x4 → 5x5 with more tiles to remember!

💡 Pro Tip: Group tiles into geometric shapes (corners, lines, clusters) rather than individual squares!
🚦 Sequential Memory Guide

How to Play Sequence Recall

1
Watch & Listen:

The glowing neon pads flash and chime a musical sequence of colors.

2
Repeat the Sequence:

When "👆 Your Turn!" appears, tap the pads in the exact same chronological order.

3
Expanding Chain:

Each round adds +1 new step to the sequence and speeds up tempo!

💡 Pro Tip: Hum the musical chime notes or whisper the colors in your head as they play!
